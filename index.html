Cat House Strategy Game (2 Player Local)

Caller chooses a number. Drawer must connect from current point to selected target without crossing lines or touching other nodes.

Available numbers

Turn

How turns work
  • Player 1 calls, Player 2 draws.
  • Then Player 2 calls, Player 1 draws.
  • Repeat until all numbers are used.
  • Final move: connect the last point back to Cat House.
